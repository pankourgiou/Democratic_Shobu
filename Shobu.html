Democratic Shobu

Real Shobu rules, but nobody decides alone. Your seven advisors put a ballot in front of you; the opposing council debates, changes its mind, and votes on every move using the program you choose.

Shobu

  • Four 4×4 boards. You (ivory) start at the bottom, the council (dark) at the top, 4 stones per board.
  • Passive move on one of your two home boards: slide a stone 1–2 squares in any of 8 directions. No pushing.
  • Aggressive move with the identical direction and distance on a board of the opposite shade (either side of the table). You may push one enemy stone, never two or your own. A pushed stone moves one square on, or falls off the board.
  • Win by clearing all enemy stones from any one board.

The democratic layer

  • Seven advisors, each with a temperament: Hawk hunts, Guardian protects, Strategist holds the centre, Mason avoids edges, Opportunist spots collapses, Trickster has hunches, Pragmatist balances.
  • On your turn they propose a ballot. Vote for an option, or write in any legal move yourself.
  • On the council's turn, advisors propose, can be swayed toward the leader, then the chosen program decides: plurality, Borda, approval, rotating dictator or lottery.
